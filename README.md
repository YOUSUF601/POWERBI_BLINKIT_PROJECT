# POWERBI_BLINKIT_PROJECT


# 📊 Blinkit Sales & Performance Analysis Dashboard – Power BI Project

## 🔎 Project Overview

This Power BI dashboard provides a comprehensive analysis of Blinkit's sales performance, outlet distribution, product categories, and customer ratings. The objective of this project is to monitor business performance, identify sales trends, and support strategic decision-making using interactive data visualization.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

* Analyze total sales and average sales performance.
* Understand item distribution across outlet types and sizes.
* Evaluate customer satisfaction through ratings.
* Identify top-performing product categories.
* Compare performance across different outlet tiers and locations.

---

## 📌 Key KPIs (Top-Level Metrics)

* **Total Sales:** 1.20M
* **Average Sales:** 140.99
* **Number of Items:** 9K
* **Average Rating:** 3.92

These KPIs provide a quick snapshot of overall business health.

---

## 📈 Dashboard Features & Insights

### 1️⃣ Sales Trend by Outlet Establishment Year

* Sales increased significantly after 2017.
* Peak performance observed around 2018–2019.
* Slight decline during later years, indicating potential external or market impact factors.

---

### 2️⃣ Sales by Item Type

* Top-performing categories:

  * Fruits & Snacks (~0.18M each)
  * Household & Frozen products (~0.14M–0.12M)
* Lower-performing categories:

  * Seafood, Breakfast, Starch

This helps identify high-demand product segments.

---

### 3️⃣ Fat Content Analysis

* Regular fat products generate higher revenue compared to low-fat items.
* Useful for inventory planning and customer preference analysis.

---

### 4️⃣ Outlet Size Performance

* Medium-sized outlets contribute the highest sales (~507.9K).
* Followed by Small and High outlets.
* Indicates optimal performance from medium store formats.

---

### 5️⃣ Outlet Location Type Analysis

* Tier 3 locations generate the highest revenue (472K+).
* Tier 2 follows closely.
* Tier 1 has comparatively lower contribution.

This insight supports location-based expansion strategies.

---

### 6️⃣ Outlet Type Performance Comparison

Comparison across:

* Supermarket Type1
* Supermarket Type2
* Supermarket Type3
* Grocery Stores

🔹 Supermarket Type1 leads in:

* Total Sales (787.55K)
* Number of Items (5577)
* Item Visibility

🔹 Grocery stores generate lower sales but maintain competitive ratings.
